## Other images

- **Unsplash:** some fallback and recipe photos are loaded from `images.unsplash.com`, and `UNSPLASH_ACCESS_KEY` is used for image search. These are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Photographer credits are not recorded in this repo.
- **TheMealDB:** a few recipe images and the recipe data are fetched from [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com/api.php).
- **Local dish photos** (`frontend/src/assets/images/`): the source and licence of these files were not recorded when they were added. They are included for academic demonstration only. If you own one of these photos and want it credited or removed, please open an issue.

## Data

- **Restaurant menu dataset** (`backend/data/restaurants.csv`): restaurant and menu listings compiled from publicly available online listings, for academic use only. The original source was not recorded. This data is not owned by the project authors.
- **Nutrition table** (`backend/data/nutrition_master.csv`): per-dish values compiled by the project team from public nutrition references. These are approximate and not medical advice.

## APIs and services

Live data comes from WeatherAPI (weather), Geoapify and OpenStreetMap (places and geocoding, data © OpenStreetMap contributors under the ODbL), TheMealDB (recipes), Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) and Google Gemini (LLM features).
