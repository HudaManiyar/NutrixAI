# Attributions

Nutrix AI is an academic project. This file credits the third-party images, data and services it uses.

## Images

- **Dish photos** (`frontend/src/assets/images/`): sourced from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which allow free use without attribution. Some images were generated with Google AI Studio.
- **Fallback photos:** dishes without a local photo use images loaded from the Unsplash API, under the Unsplash License.

## Data

- **Recipes:** some recipes are fetched from [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com/api.php); others come from `backend/data/curated_recipes.json`.
- **Restaurant menu dataset** (`backend/data/restaurants.csv`): compiled from publicly available online listings, for academic use only. 
- **Nutrition table** (`backend/data/nutrition_master.csv`): approximate per-dish values compiled, Not medical advice.

## APIs and services

Live data comes from [WeatherAPI](https://www.weatherapi.com) (weather), [Geoapify](https://www.geoapify.com) and OpenStreetMap (places and geocoding; map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), TheMealDB (recipes), Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) and Google Gemini (LLM).
